How it works

Three simple steps, a few seconds a day.

  1. Set your rates onceSalary per day, overtime per hour, and your currency (for example Rs).
  2. Mark each dayTap P when you work. Add overtime hours if you stayed late.
  3. Check your totalYour 15-day and monthly salary update instantly. Compare it with your pay slip.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the daily wage and overtime calculator.

Is AR Salary Tracker free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no ads and no sign-up.

Does the app need internet?

No. After you install it, the app works fully offline.

Is my salary data private?

Yes. Your data is saved only on your own phone and is never uploaded. Use Download backup in the app to keep a copy, for example on Google Drive.

My daily wage increased. Will my old days change?

No. Type the new rate at the top and choose the date it starts. Days before that date keep the old rate, and the app shows your full rate history.

How is overtime calculated?

Overtime pay = overtime hours × your overtime rate per hour. It is added to that day's wage and to your 15-day and monthly totals.

Why does my phone show a warning when installing?

The app is not on the Play Store, so Android warns about it. Tap More details → Install anyway.

Does it work on iPhone or a computer?

The app file is for Android 8 or newer. On iPhone or a computer, open the online version in your browser. Data is saved in that browser.
